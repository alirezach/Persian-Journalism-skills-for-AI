# Structure, transitions and rhythm

Diagnose the relation between sentences before changing the surface rhythm. Uniform length, repeated openings and formulaic closures can make prose tedious, but variation is not a numerical target.

## Paragraph logic

Identify what each paragraph does: reports a finding, defines a concept, introduces a condition, supplies evidence, contrasts claims or states an interpretation. Remove a generic opening only if the substantive point remains. Preserve a conclusion that is actually supported; do not add a new takeaway simply because a paragraph needs an ending.

Repeated همچنین or علاوه بر این may be unnecessary when the relation is already clear. Do not replace an additive relation with بنابراین or در نتیجه unless the source establishes that inference. A tacked-on «که نشان‌دهندهٔ ... است» may contain real analysis; decide whether it is empty repetition, an attributed interpretation or an unsupported inference before changing it.

## Lists and headings

Keep an actual three-part classification intact. A reflexive triad of near-synonymous praise can be reduced, but the number three is not the problem. Use lists for real parallel items or procedures and prose for connected reasoning. Preserve user-required formatting, academic conventions and substantive headings. A bold label is not inherently wrong.

## Sentence rhythm

Let complexity determine sentence length. Split an overloaded sentence at a real boundary while preserving a condition or attribution. Avoid artificially short dramatic fragments or forcing alternating long and short sentences. Preserve genuine parenthetical asides and meaningful repetition; do not add jokes, anecdotes, hesitation or personal confession to simulate humanity.

## Imported constructions

Repair a false «از ... تا ...» range only when its endpoints do not represent a real scale or span. Preserve actual historical ranges and trajectories. «نه‌تنها ... بلکه ...» can be useful; simplify repeated rhetorical scaffolding without dropping either proposition. Distinguish unnecessary punctuation rhythm from quotation, formula and range notation. Never change protected strings to satisfy a style cue.

## Delivery discipline

A smoother arrangement is acceptable only within the requested scope. Detox does not authorize summarizing an article, changing its intended audience, adding explanatory facts or reorganizing an entire argument without permission. If a transition cannot be repaired without inventing a premise, leave the relevant passage and flag the missing connection.
