# Register, voice and audience

Choose the register before changing phrasing. Explicit requested register comes first; otherwise use the deliverable, its audience and the author's existing voice. A casual request for a proposal still calls for professional prose. If high-stakes signals conflict, ask one focused question rather than silently guessing.

## Distinct registers

- Professional: full written verb forms, precise claims and direct syntax. Warmth can come from clarity; it does not require slang, jokes or fabricated intimacy.
- Administrative: retain functional forms of address, a concise greeting, a clear request and an appropriate closing. Remove stacked ceremony only when it adds no function. A conventional polite request is not a machine signature.
- Academic: keep disciplinary terms, necessary passive constructions, conventional headings and meaningful uncertainty. Formality is not inflation. Do not convert the author's argument into promotional or conversational prose.
- Public explanatory writing: readable written Persian with appropriate directness. Explain only what the source supports; an invented example presented as real is not clarification.
- Colloquial written text: use consistent forms only when the audience and brief call for them. A quoted speaker can retain a different register. Do not insert «راستش»، «خب» or slang merely to simulate a person.
- Literary and personal writing: preserve intentional rhythm, metaphor and genuine idiosyncrasy. Do not flatten poetry, character speech or historically meaningful wording.

## Voice is not register

Maintain the distinction between impersonal facts, direct address to the reader and the author's first-person experience. Use «ما» only for actions actually attributed to that author or organization. Do not invent years of experience, clients, feelings, memories or achievements. Repetition of «شما» can be removed where the verb already carries the address, but not where emphasis or contrast is intended.

## No automatic tone conversion

A platform name alone does not establish informality; a public institutional post may need formal wording. Preserve required disclosure statements and meaningful courtesy. One expression, exclamation or formal verb is not evidence of authorship. If the text already reads naturally and appropriately, keep it.

Synthetic contrast: a formal proposal can say «پیشنهاد می‌کنیم جلسه‌ای کوتاه برگزار شود» without becoming chatty. It should not become «یه جلسه بذاریم، همه‌چی حله» unless that register was actually requested and the promise is supported.
