# Academic and evidence-sensitive detox

A polished sentence can be less faithful than an awkward one. Source fidelity outranks elegance, paragraph symmetry and stylistic variety.

## Preserve every claim's strength

Keep quantities, precision, population, period, conditions, negation, uncertainty and attribution. حدود is not optional padding. «در این نمونه» must not become «در ایران». «ممکن است» cannot become «ثابت می‌کند», and a firm finding should not acquire unsupported hedges. Preserve necessary/sufficient, at least/at most, some/all and before/after distinctions.

Do not turn association into causation, a non-significant result into no effect, or no effect into equivalence. Odds, probability, rates and percentage points are different. A source's negative statement remains negative even if a style preference favors positive sentences. Do not round a number to make prose smoother.

## Genre conventions

A thesis may legitimately have مقدمه، روش، یافته‌ها and بحث. A scientific passive can focus on the procedure when the actor is irrelevant or unknown. Retain a real technical term and repeat it consistently; synonym cycling can change the concept. Do not replace an argument with bold-label bullets, but preserve hypotheses, numbered procedures and genuine lists.

## Source comparison

Compare definitions, outcome, population, time, measurement and claim strength before accepting asserted agreement or disagreement. A study of رضایت اداری and a study of اعتماد نهادی need not contradict one another. Conversely, two explicit opposing claims about the same outcome and context should not be obscured by a generic caution. If information is insufficient, preserve both statements and the author's interpretation and flag the exact uncertainty outside the prose when notes are allowed.

A source list is not automatically a synthesis. Reorganizing supplied claims cannot justify inventing a missing bridge, literature trend, theoretical gap or causal mechanism. The author's inference remains the author's inference, not a finding retroactively attributed to a cited paper.

## Citations and missing evidence

Keep provided names, years, page locators, links and quotation boundaries. Do not invent a DOI or fill a vague «مطالعات نشان می‌دهد» with a plausible citation. Removing the attribution can turn an unsupported reported claim into an unqualified fact, so retain and flag it when necessary. Do not claim to have read a source merely because its citation appears in the text.

## Specificity and disclosure

Replace empty praise with actual supplied details, not invented statistics or illustrative facts passed off as findings. If no details are available, reduce empty promotion and note a consequential gap rather than manufacture evidence. Preserve AI-assistance disclosures required by the user or institution. This skill does not authenticate authorship, defeat a detector or certify compliance with a journal's policy.
