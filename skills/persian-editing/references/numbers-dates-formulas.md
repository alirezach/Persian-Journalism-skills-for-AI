# Transcription, numbers, formulas, dates and abbreviations

## Pronunciation and transcription

Phonetic marks represent sound, not decorative punctuation. Preserve the transcription system and contrasts actually provided. Do not invent pronunciations, stress or source-language spellings. If a notation is inconsistent, identify the inconsistency and ask for the intended scheme where it affects interpretation.

## Numbers and units

Determine whether a token is a measured quantity, count, ordinal, identifier, version, date, interval, fraction or textual label. Persian digits are a prose-display preference, not permission to change machine tokens. Preserve precision, signs, bounds, order, denominator and units. Spelling out a number must preserve exactly the same value; do not automatically spell out every small number in technical material.

Keep percent distinct from percentage points, total from per-capita, mean from median, person from household, nominal from real, and stock from flow. Zero, missing, suppressed and not applicable remain distinct. A measurement without a unit needs a query, not an invented unit. Large numbers, decimals and leading-zero identifiers require deliberate handling.

Use the selected decimal and grouping conventions only when interpretation is certain. The compilation has slash-based examples; do not confuse a slash with a decimal separator, a date boundary or division. Do not round, recompute, change currency or convert calendars as ordinary editing.

## Formulas and statistical notation

Preserve the whole expression, its variables, subscripts, signs and inequality direction. Separate a display correction from mathematical correction. Do not reverse an expression to match paragraph direction. Keep a negative sign attached to its value and an interval's endpoints in their intended order. A p-value, confidence interval or odds ratio does not grant a new substantive conclusion.

## Dates and chronology

Preserve the original calendar, completeness and uncertainty of a date. A year alone is not a day-specific timestamp. Do not infer a missing century or apply a rough solar/Gregorian offset as exact conversion. If conversion is explicitly requested, it is a separate calculation requiring an appropriate method and recorded original. Do not repair a historical chronology from memory.

## Abbreviations

Expand only when the expansion is supplied or reliably established for this context. Keep Latin abbreviations and technical case intact. Define an abbreviation at first use if the full form is available and the task permits that local editorial clarification; never invent the underlying term. Ensure the same abbreviation does not silently name two different entities.

Synthetic invariant: «از ۳۲ درصد به ۲۴ درصد» is an eight-percentage-point change, but correcting or explaining the arithmetic requires explicit scope. An editor can flag a contradictory description without silently altering the author's number. `NA`, `0` and `0012` in data/code remain byte-identical.
