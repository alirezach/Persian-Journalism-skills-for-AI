# Grammar and syntax: local intervention, preserved argument

## Sentence completeness and attachment

Identify the subject, predicate, complements and dependent clauses before moving words. Repair a fragment only if the missing grammatical relation is recoverable from the text; do not supply a missing factual premise. For long sentences, split at a genuine boundary and carry the relevant condition, attribution and qualifier with its claim. Do not turn sequence into causality by adding بنابراین or زیرا.

Resolve a pronoun only when one antecedent is clear. Modifier attachment, coordination and scope of negation need special care: moving فقط، حتی، نیز or a negative can change the assertion. Preserve the distinction between the author's reasoning and the cited source's finding. A logical inconsistency is a query, not permission to rewrite the conclusion.

## Agreement and verbs

Check subject-verb agreement in context, including collective and inanimate subjects and accepted Persian usage. Do not apply an inflexible plural rule without considering meaning. Keep tense, aspect, mood, person and evidential status. A proposal, intention, attempt and completed act are not interchangeable.

Inspect descriptive participial constructions for shared subject and time. Repair a dangling construction only when the intended agent is known. Recover an omitted verb only when the elliptical relation is unambiguous; otherwise flag it. Avoid needless repetition of a verb, but retain it when omission changes tense, polarity or coordination.

Passive constructions are legitimate when focus is the process or the agent is unknown. Use an active construction only when the agent is supplied and the change preserves emphasis. Do not invent a committee, ministry or researcher merely to make a sentence active. Preserve the conventional meaning of compound verbs; shortening them is not an automatic improvement.

## Prepositions, را and conjunctions

Check the complement required by the verb or expression. Do not translate an English preposition word-for-word when Persian syntax differs. Place را with the intended definite object, not a neighboring clause; do not add it to every noun phrase. Correct preposition omission or duplication only after establishing which words depend on which head.

Conjunctions must express the actual relationship: addition, alternative, contrast, condition or consequence. In parallel constructions, preserve both propositions. A three-item list can be valid. Different outcomes, populations or periods can make a claimed disagreement unclear; retain the statements and query the comparison rather than automatically reconciling them.

## Negative and statistical statements

Keep «تفاوت معناداری مشاهده نشد» distinct from «تفاوتی وجود ندارد» and «دو گروه برابرند». Keep association distinct from causation and a confidence interval distinct from a range of observed values. Do not weaken a firm source claim or harden a qualified one. Positive wording is a style preference, not a reason to remove a negative result.

## Final comparison

For each substantial sentence edit, identify the same agent, action/state, object, time, condition, scope and strength before and after. If any cannot be matched, revert and flag the unresolved point. Check the surrounding paragraph: a locally grammatical sentence can still attach a conclusion to the wrong evidence.
