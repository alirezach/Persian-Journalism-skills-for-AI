## Conflict resolutions

- The compilation often uses خانه‌ی; the project default stays خانهٔ, with explicit consistent alternatives allowed.
- Slash-based numeric conventions must be interpreted before display conversion. A slash can mean a date separator, fraction, alternative or notation, not necessarily a decimal.
- Long-quotation thresholds differ within the compilation. Treat them as house-style alternatives rather than contradictory mandatory rules.
- Positive wording, active voice, short sentences and Persian equivalents are contextual preferences. They cannot override negation, uncertainty, event stage, unknown agency, established terminology or quoted evidence.
- Correcting scientific content, removing unsupported arguments, adding missing information and comparing a translation with its original are outside editing-only scope. Flag the need, do not perform the intervention.
- Names and dates cannot be corrected by plausible guessing. Calendar conversion is not ordinary proofreading.
- Book-preparation principles can guide consistency checks; actual layout and pagination require actual pages.
