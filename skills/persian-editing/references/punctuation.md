# Persian punctuation: role before replacement

Apply these rules to editable prose only. Punctuation can change logical scope; first identify the sentence structure, quotation boundary and intended relation. Default spacing: no space before a sentence mark, one before the next word; no unnecessary space just inside paired marks. Paragraph boundaries, adjacent closing marks, nested quotation marks and structured syntax are exceptions to a mechanical spacing rule.

| Mark | Editorial decision | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| نقطه . | Close a complete sentence; distinguish sentence end from abbreviation or decimal. | Do not split a condition from its consequence or append punctuation inside code. |
| ویرگول ، | Separate list items, parenthetical material or clauses when needed for reading. | Do not detach an ordinary subject from its predicate or a complement from its verb. |
| نقطه‌ویرگول ؛ | Separate closely related independent clauses or complex list items. | It is not an all-purpose substitute for a comma or proof of causality. |
| دونقطه : | Introduce an actual explanation, quotation or specified list. | Do not insert it after an unfinished syntactic fragment merely for visual emphasis. |
| پرسش ؟ | Mark a direct question. | An indirect report of a question does not automatically take a question mark. |
| تعجب ! | Preserve intended emotion or exclamation appropriate to genre. | Do not add enthusiasm to academic or documentary prose. |
| گیومه « » | Delimit exact quoted wording or a term where convention calls for it. | Never silently change the quoted words; distinguish term labels from speech. |
| سه‌نقطه … | Distinguish hesitation, interruption, unfinished enumeration, source omission and a form placeholder. | Do not create omissions or reconstruct missing words; normalize appearance only when role is known. |
| پرانتز ( ) | Enclose genuinely supplementary material. | Preserve essential qualifications; nested pairs require a consistent scheme. |
| قلاب [ ] | Mark editorial interpolation or a different nesting level when appropriate. | An interpolation is not the source author's wording and must not invent facts. |
| خط فاصله | Classify dialogue, parenthesis, range, route, compound or machine token. | No blanket replacement; never hand-split Persian words to force a line break. |
| خط مورب / | Distinguish alternatives, dates, fractions, phonetic notation and identifiers. | Do not guess whether a slash means and, or, per, or a decimal convention. |
| پیکان ← | Preserve a genuine direction, transformation or logical relation. | Do not reverse it simply because prose is RTL. |
| ستاره * | Keep footnote or special annotation roles consistent. | Do not treat Markdown emphasis or a multiplication symbol as a footnote. |
| ایضاً | Repetition mark in an established table or transcription. | Expansion requires an unambiguous referenced value; do not fabricate it. |
| خط موازی || | Preserve the role in notation, dictionaries or specialized display. | It can be an operator in code; do not normalize as prose. |
| ابرو ~ | Identify approximation, relation or specialized notation. | Approximation changes certainty; do not delete it as decoration. |
| خط بلند/زیرخط | Identify blank fields, typographic rules or source notation. | A blank field is not missing prose to fill; underscores in identifiers are immutable. |

## Paired marks and quotation punctuation

Balance pairs only when their intended span is clear. Determine whether a punctuation mark belongs to the quoted sentence or the surrounding sentence before moving it. Preserve exact documentary quotations even if their spelling or punctuation looks unusual; flag a suspected problem separately when notes are allowed.

Synthetic example: «نتیجه روشن است : داده کافی نیست .» becomes «نتیجه روشن است: داده کافی نیست.» in eligible prose. If this is a quoted example of an error, keep it as supplied. A numeric sequence containing dots or slashes requires classification before any substitution.

The compilation gives more than one threshold for setting long quotations apart. Treat those as house-style alternatives, not simultaneous universal rules. See the documentation guide for choosing a consistent convention without altering quotation content.
