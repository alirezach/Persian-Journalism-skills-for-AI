# Lexical, idiomatic and collocational editing

## Meaning before preference

Identify the intended sense, collocation and register. An editorial replacement must preserve the proposition, event stage and degree of assertion. A shorter verb is not necessarily equivalent: عرضه‌کردن is not فروختن; قصدکردن is not انجام‌دادن; شنیدن is not مشاهده‌کردن. A specific classification expressed by محسوب می‌شود need not be a removable copula.

## Foreign influence and equivalents

Inspect literal translations of idioms, imported prepositions and collocations that obscure meaning. Prefer an established idiomatic expression of the same register. Do not invent Persian equivalents or reject an established loan merely because it is foreign. The compilation's equivalents appendix is a contextual candidate list, not a mandatory dictionary. Record an unresolved specialist equivalent as a query.

## Persian expressions

Correct a misused word only when its intended meaning is recoverable. Distinguish an intentional metaphor, a literary voice, a conventional legal term and an accidental mixed metaphor. Do not replace an author's evaluation with the editor's. Avoid broad claims that a historically attested or accepted alternative is universally wrong.

## Redundancy

Delete only duplicated wording with no semantic or pragmatic function. Keep repetition needed for terminology, contrast, scope, emphasis or readability. A qualification such as حدود، احتمالاً، در این نمونه or به گفتهٔ is not padding. A word that appears twice can carry two different roles. Negative findings cannot be made positive simply to simplify their surface form.

## Arabic forms in Persian

Review plural endings, gender agreement, tanvin and borrowed constructions in the context of Persian usage. Do not impose Arabic grammatical agreement on a naturalized Persian noun, or remove every Arabic loan. False tanvin on Persian words can be corrected in ordinary prose; legitimate loans and protected quotations stay intact. Orthographic preferences must not alter quoted evidence.

## Lexical checklist

Does the replacement keep the same sense and referent? Does it preserve necessary/sufficient, some/all, before/after, possibility/certainty and offer/completion? Is the term consistent across the document without forcing one translation for different senses? Is the expression correct for this genre? If the answer is unclear, retain it and explain the issue when notes are allowed.

Synthetic example: «این بخش به بررسی تعریف شاخص می‌پردازد» may become «این بخش تعریف شاخص را بررسی می‌کند». But «این شاخص از معتبرترین شاخص‌هاست» must not become «این شاخص معتبرترین است». This module corrects language; it does not classify phrases as AI signatures.
