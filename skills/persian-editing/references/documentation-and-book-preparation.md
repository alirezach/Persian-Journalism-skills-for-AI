# Documentation and preparation of a written work

Inspect only material actually supplied. This module checks editorial consistency, not bibliographic authenticity, copyright permission or finished typesetting.

## Quotations and citations

Distinguish verbatim quotation, paraphrase and the author's interpretation. Preserve exact quotation content and attribution. Missing quotation marks or an uncertain boundary require clarification if more than one reading is possible. A quote without a source is not an invitation to invent author, year or page. Keep a cited page range and edition tied to the source actually named.

Follow the requested citation style consistently; do not impose one universal format on every discipline. The compilation gives differing long-quote thresholds, including line-count and word-count formulations. Select the applicable house style rather than applying both; reformatting never authorizes shortening the quote. Editorial insertions must remain visibly editorial and supported.

## Footnotes and endnotes

Check marker order, missing or duplicate markers, and the relation between note and body text. A note can contain an equivalent, explanation or reference, but its content must be supplied, not fabricated. Distinguish a missing note from an empty placeholder. Renumbering must update every actual reference in the available scope; disclose incomplete coverage.

## Structure and book furniture

Check the consistency of title/subtitle, author information, foreword/preface, contents, heading hierarchy, chapters, appendices, glossary, bibliography and index where present. Do not create missing prefatory material or rewrite the argument to fit a template. Distinguish chapter titles from running heads and captions from main prose.

For tables and figures, check label, title, unit, source, notes and cross-reference consistency. Do not alter underlying values or invent an unseen figure. Caption placement is a house-style matter; page positioning and legibility need actual layout inspection. An index entry needs a real occurrence and verified locator; never infer page numbers from a plain-text file.

## Bibliographies and electronic sources

Preserve the author, title, edition, publisher, date and locator supplied. Identify unmatched citations or incomplete fields, but do not complete them from plausible guesses. Retain the exact URL/DOI and distinguish access date from publication date. A working link does not prove source reliability; an inaccessible link does not prove a citation false.

## Delivery

Separate language edits, consistency queries and layout items not checked. For a proof-stage request, inspect supplied pages and report the real extent of checking. Do not announce a publication-ready book from a body-text pass alone. The source's substantive and scientific editing duties are outside this skill: report gaps without adding or deleting content on the author's behalf.
