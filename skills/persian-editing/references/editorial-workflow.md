# Professional editorial workflow

## Intake and responsibility

Establish the available text, intended use, genre, explicit scope and any house style. Editing serves the author's intended meaning and readers, not the editor's preferred ideology or voice. A publisher's supplied style can govern presentation, but cannot override the user's authorization or change evidence. Conflicting instructions that affect meaning require a focused query; harmless unspecified typography can use the documented default.

Keep the original separately and make a working copy. Identify immutable spans before global operations. The source compilation's discussion of substantive editing, scientific correction, deleting unsupported material and adding missing content describes a broader profession: these interventions are not authorized by this editing-only skill. Report their need rather than performing them.

## Four passes

1. Map the text: sections, argumentative roles, quotations, notes, captions, tables and references actually available. Identify repeated terms and names.
2. Perform the requested technical or language work using the relevant reference modules. Maintain a compact style ledger when the document is long or inconsistent.
3. Inspect consistency across the processed scope: forms of names, terminology, heading levels, reference keys, table/figure labels, units and abbreviations. Do not invent missing pages or citations.
4. Compare with the original for completeness and meaning, then read the edited text for clarity. Revert any unsafe simplification. Distinguish findings, attributed claims, interpretation and recommendations.

## Decision classes

- Clear mechanical error: correct in eligible prose.
- Consistent acceptable variant: keep unless a house style was requested.
- Local language error with one recoverable meaning: correct minimally.
- Several plausible meanings or uncertain name: preserve and query.
- Factual or logical problem: flag with location, not a guessed correction.
- Missing source or unseen page: disclose the limit, not a completed check.

## Final checklist

Has every supplied section in the agreed scope been processed? Are all numbers, signs, units, dates, conditions, negations and attribution preserved? Are direct quotations and machine strings unchanged? Are glossary choices consistent without forcing one meaning onto a polysemous word? Do comparisons concern comparable populations and constructs? Are unresolved issues separated from the author's prose? Does the output obey a text-only request? Is every claimed check something actually performed?

A finished language pass does not establish factual correctness, legal review, source authenticity, publication permission or final layout quality. Do not mark human approval, page inspection or software execution as complete without evidence.
