# Contextual Persian AI detox

Adapted from `ali2000hos/persian-writing`, especially writing-style.md T1-T18 and academic.md. See [source decisions](source-decisions.md) and [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The target is clearer prose, not concealing authorship or satisfying a detector.

## First determine register

Explicit requested register comes first. Otherwise use the deliverable, audience and existing voice, not the informality of the user's chat message. A proposal needs professional language; a thesis needs disciplinary precision; an administrative letter may need conventional courtesy. Do not force colloquial Persian into any of them. In a mixed deliverable, captions and a formal cover letter may legitimately differ.

## Eighteen contextual pattern groups

| ID | Inspect | Safe response |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Inflated copulas and nominal verbs: می‌باشد، محسوب می‌شود | Prefer است or a direct verb only when classification, tense and emphasis remain equivalent. |
| T2 | Announcement filler: لازم به ذکر است، شایان ذکر است | State the actual point when the announcement serves no function. |
| T3 | Unsupported significance: نقش بسزایی، تحولی شگرف | Preserve any real claim; remove empty promotion or flag missing support. Do not invent an effect. |
| T4 | Generic openings and closings: در دنیای امروز | Start with an existing substantive point; retain genuine time context. Do not add a new conclusion. |
| T5 | Habitual در راستای، در جهت نیل به | Use a direct relation such as برای only where purpose is actually meant. |
| T6 | Promotional superlatives without evidence | Retain documented specifics; do not manufacture client counts, experience, measurements or achievements. |
| T7 | Reflexive three-item lists | Remove redundancy, not items just to change the count. A real three-part classification stays. |
| T8 | Automatic نه‌تنها ... بلکه and decorative contrasts | Simplify rhetorical scaffolding while retaining both propositions and their actual relationship. |
| T9 | Tacked-on interpretation: که نشان‌دهندهٔ ... است | Keep supported analysis and attribution; do not erase real interpretation or fabricate evidence for it. |
| T10 | Vague authority: کارشناسان معتقدند | Use a supplied source if available; otherwise flag missing attribution. Do not turn an attributed claim into an unqualified fact. |
| T11 | Decorative از ... تا ... ranges | Use an ordinary list if no meaningful range is intended; preserve actual scales and historical spans. |
| T12 | Imported punctuation rhythm and excessive dashes | Prefer Persian sentence structure and suitable punctuation where clarity improves; protect quoted punctuation. |
| T13 | Calques and translationese | Choose an idiomatic phrase of the same register and meaning, not a loose colloquial approximation. |
| T14 | Repeated همچنین، علاوه بر این | Retain the logical link, but remove redundant connectors where the relation is already clear. |
| T15 | False tanvin: گاهاً، دوماً، ناچاراً | In ordinary prose use گاهی، دوم، به‌ناچار as context permits. Keep legitimate Arabic loans and protected quotations. |
| T16 | Bold-label bullets replacing connected prose | Use paragraphs for an argument, lists for actual lists. Preserve task-required formatting and meaningful headings. |
| T17 | Chatbot residue: سؤال خوبی است، طبق درخواست شما | Remove only when it is accidental residue, not legitimate correspondence, quoted dialogue or required disclosure. |
| T18 | Synonym cycling, hidden agency, stacked hedges, manufactured drama, slogans and decorative emojis | Keep terminology stable; preserve necessary hedges and unknown agency. Avoid both uniform templates and artificial staccato. |

## Academic and evidence-sensitive writing

Conventional headings, an appropriate passive, a precise technical term and a meaningful qualification are not defects. Keep scope, quantities and evidential strength exactly: حدود، حداقل، در این نمونه، ممکن است and statistically meaningful distinctions matter. Do not add hedges to a firm source claim either.

Preserve the distinction between a cited source's finding and the author's interpretation. Rewriting a literature paragraph does not authorize synthesizing new sources or supplying a missing causal bridge. A missing source stays missing, with a note when allowed. Keep the user's citation style; do not impose one universal academic format.

## What not to do

Do not ban every occurrence of a phrase, three-part list, formal verb or technical term. Isolated stylistic markers do not establish authorship. Do not invent first-person memories, anecdotes, numbers, quotations or citations to add specificity. Do not add mistakes, hidden characters, arbitrary sentence-length variation or misleading authorship claims. Preserve required disclosure statements.

## Synthetic, source-faithful example

Input: «لازم به ذکر است که گزارش در روز دوشنبه منتشر شد و شامل سه جدول است.»

Revision: «گزارش روز دوشنبه منتشر شد و سه جدول دارد.»

The event, day and quantity are unchanged. Do not add a date, publisher or explanation. If the sentence is quoted verbatim, preserve it.

## Final pass

Read for natural phrasing, then compare every claim with the input. Keep genuine asides and voice; remove only empty scaffolding. Better rhythm should follow clearer content, not a statistical target. For a combined request, continue with [language editing](language-editing.md) as needed and [technical editing](technical-editing.md), without changing the chosen register.
