# Conservative Persian language editing

Source scope: register, lexical and grammatical sections of the supplied Zolfaghari compilation. These are contextual editorial principles, not an unconditional substitution dictionary.

## Order of decisions

1. Understand the proposition, agent, time, condition and intended audience.
2. Preserve the author's register and perspective unless a change is requested.
3. Fix a local ambiguity or grammatical problem with the smallest sufficient change.
4. Simplify only when the shorter construction has the same meaning.
5. Re-read against the source, especially where a sentence was split or combined.

## What to inspect

- **Word choice:** distinguish near-synonyms and their collocations. Prefer an established, precise term to an ornamental substitute. Persian equivalents are candidates, not a requirement to replace every loanword. Keep one term for one concept in technical prose.
- **Redundancy:** remove genuinely duplicated wording, not evidential detail, emphasis meaningful to the author, a contrast, a definition or a necessary restatement. Shortening is not summarization.
- **Grammar:** inspect agreement, verb complements, prepositions, pronoun reference, the object marker را, modifiers and the attachment of dependent clauses. A debatable construction is not an automatic error.
- **Verb structure:** prefer direct verbs when equivalent. Distinguish an event from an offer, attempt, intention or ongoing process. Preserve tense, aspect and modality.
- **Voice and agency:** use an active construction only when the actor is known and the change preserves emphasis and attribution. Scientific passive constructions can be appropriate. Never supply an actor merely to avoid a passive.
- **Sentence length:** split a crowded sentence only where its logical relations remain clear. Preserve the relation between a qualifier and its claim. Do not create a causal transition from mere sequence.
- **Cohesion:** make an existing connection clearer, but do not invent the missing argument. Resolve a pronoun only when its antecedent is unambiguous. Preserve disagreement rather than smoothing it into consensus.
- **Register:** professional and academic prose can be clear without slang. Retain useful administrative formulas in administrative letters. Preserve intentional literary rhythm; do not standardize quoted poetry or a character's speech.

## Semantic traps

| Input meaning | Unsafe simplification | Required decision |
|---|---|---|
| در معرض فروش قرار داد | فروخت | Offering is not completed sale. Preserve the distinction. |
| تفاوت معناداری مشاهده نشد | دو گروه یکسان بودند | Absence of significance is not demonstrated equivalence. |
| با پیامد ارتباط داشت | باعث پیامد شد | Association is not causation. |
| یکی از رایج‌ترین روش‌ها | رایج‌ترین روش | Do not strengthen a ranking. |
| احتمالاً در این نمونه | به‌طور کلی | Preserve uncertainty and population scope. |
| تصمیم گرفته شد | مدیر تصمیم گرفت | Do not invent the decision-maker. |

A preference for positive sentences cannot erase a negative finding. A preference for brevity cannot delete a caveat. A style rule against a particular hedge may permit an equivalent phrase, but never a different degree of certainty.

## Synthetic example

Input: «این بخش به بررسی شیوهٔ گردآوری داده‌ها می‌پردازد.»

Conservative revision: «این بخش شیوهٔ گردآوری داده‌ها را بررسی می‌کند.»

No new method, source, result or claim is supplied. If the text were a protected quotation, leave it unchanged.

## Unresolved issues

For an uncertain fact, conflicting figures, an unclear referent or a possible source error: preserve the relevant passage and add a separate concise note when output constraints allow. Correcting content, fact-checking, abridging and publication require their own explicit scope. Finish with [technical editing](technical-editing.md) when the requested mode includes it.
